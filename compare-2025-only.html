Sources and year definitions

2025 figures only. This page deliberately excludes 2024 average-sales figures. Recent reports use different reporting dates and definitions for store counts, so figures here are identified as the 2025 numbers reported by the sources below. COMPOSE COFFEE’s comparable 2025 average annual store sales were not available in the cited report; no 2024 value has been substituted.
  1. Leaders Fact — 2026 franchise status report based on FTC disclosure data. Reports 2025 store counts of 3,969 for MEGA MGC COFFEE and 3,325 for COMPOSE COFFEE; reports MEGA’s 2025 average annual store sales of ₩412.87 million and notes COMPOSE’s 2025 store sales were not yet published in that analysis.
  2. Seoul Economic Daily — Mega MGC Coffee Sales Hit 413 Million Won Per Store. Confirms MEGA’s 2025 average sales and year-on-year increase; also discusses the FTC’s franchise survey store-count series, which has a different reporting basis from year-end store counts.
  3. Leaders Fact — Paik’s Coffee 2025 store and sales figures. Reports 1,819 stores and average annual sales of ₩232.26 million for 2025.
