# 2025 Update: Korean Coffee Franchise Comparison

**Prepared by:** Kunwar Roshan  
**Updated:** October 21, 2026  
**Purpose:** Add a clearly sourced 2025 franchise-location snapshot to the Korean coffee franchise comparison project.

## Reported franchise locations

According to ZDNet Korea's report on the Korea Fair Trade Commission's *2025 Franchise Business Status* announcement, the coffee brands below had these reported franchise counts:

| Brand | Reported franchise locations |
|---|---:|
| MEGA MGC COFFEE | 3,325 |
| COMPOSE COFFEE | 2,649 |
| Ediya Coffee | 2,562 |
| Paik's Coffee (빽다방) | 1,712 |
| A Twosome Place | 1,510 |

**Important date note:** These are figures reported in the Commission's 2025 franchise-status release, covered by the news article in April 2026. They should not automatically be described as a live count or a verified count on December 31, 2025. Franchise disclosure figures can refer to a prior reporting period, and brands' own current store counters may use different definitions.

## How to use these numbers

- Use this table to compare **reported franchise network size**, not profitability or store-level performance.
- Do not infer that a brand with more locations earns more profit per store.
- Compare average annual store sales only when the brands' figures cover the same reporting year and use comparable calculation methods.
- Keep franchise stores distinct from company-operated stores and announced milestone store openings.

## Suggested comparison takeaway

MEGA MGC COFFEE leads this particular reported franchise-count snapshot, followed by COMPOSE COFFEE, Ediya Coffee, Paik's Coffee, and A Twosome Place. Store count is a measure of network scale only; it is not a direct measure of sales, profit, customer satisfaction, or investment suitability.
