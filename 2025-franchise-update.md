These figures are reported by ZDNet Korea in its coverage of the Korea Fair Trade Commission's **2025 franchise-business status**. They are a useful common-basis comparison of reported franchise scale; they should not be interpreted as company-owned store counts or as a measure of profitability.

## Important interpretation notes

- **Store count is not sales or profit.** A larger network does not automatically mean higher average sales or better franchisee profitability.
- **Use comparable metrics.** Do not rank one brand by store count and another by annual sales in the same ranking.
- **Sales data needs its own year and definition.** For example, MEGA MGC COFFEE's reported average annual franchise-store sales of **₩412.87 million** relate to 2025 sales and were reported in September 2026. That is a different measure from store count.
- Before using the figures in a formal academic or business report, check the underlying Fair Trade Commission disclosure and confirm the reporting period and whether the measure is franchised outlets or all outlets.
